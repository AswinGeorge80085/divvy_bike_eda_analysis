# 🚲 Divvy Bikes Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing **Divvy Bike trip data** to understand customer riding behavior, usage trends, and travel patterns between **casual riders** and **annual members**.

The analysis was performed using Python and Jupyter Notebook as part of an internship-level data analytics project. The project aims to generate meaningful business insights through data cleaning, visualization, and exploratory data analysis (EDA).

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze riding behavior of casual riders and members
- Identify the most commonly used bike types
- Study ride frequency across days and hours
- Compare trip durations between customer groups
- Discover monthly and seasonal ride trends
- Generate actionable business insights from the data

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 User Behavior
- Members use Divvy bikes more frequently than casual riders.
- Casual riders generally take longer rides compared to members.

### 🚴 Bike Preferences
- Electric bikes are among the most preferred ride types.
- Classic bikes also maintain strong usage.

### 📅 Ride Trends
- Ride activity increases during weekends.
- Peak usage hours are commonly observed during morning and evening commute times.

### 🌤️ Seasonal Trends
- Bike usage increases during warmer months, showing the impact of weather on riding activity.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

- Increase bike availability during peak hours and weekends.
- Promote membership plans to casual riders.
- Improve electric bike distribution due to high demand.
- Launch seasonal campaigns during high-usage months.
